Monitor shows changes in radio observations while your iPhone stays in one place. It is not an unattended security alarm or a count of people arriving and leaving.

Establish the starting comparison

  1. Choose Monitor, select the radio measurements you need, and keep the iPhone stationary.
  2. Keep Spectra visible and the phone unlocked. Each available source needs 30 seconds of active observation; permission waiting does not count.
  3. If useful, choose Observe 30 seconds longer once to extend the comparison to 60 seconds per source.

The starting baseline begins automatically, and the countdown follows the slowest participating source. When it is ready, change counters start at zero and the Monitor panel shows its capture time and elapsed comparison time. No duration guarantees discovery of every device. Baseline findings remain in the final inventory: initially observed does not mean trusted.

Read changes without overinterpreting them

  • Newly observed: first observed after the starting comparison, not necessarily newly arrived.
  • Returned: an identity was observed again after a gap.
  • Not recently seen: a previously observed identity is no longer being observed. For Bluetooth, the wait is 45 seconds; local-network services follow Bonjour announcements and removals.

Sleep, identity rotation, interference, and broadcast behavior can produce these changes without physical movement. The rows count distinct signal identities in the current comparison’s latest 40 retained change events. Repeated changes for one identity count once in each row; counts may decrease as older events leave this bounded history. One identity can appear in multiple rows, so adding them does not give a total device count.

Acknowledge or restart intentionally

Mark changes reviewed acknowledges the latest change without zeroing counters, trusting a device, erasing evidence, or changing the baseline. For a fresh reference, tap New Baseline in the main Monitor panel. The tap starts a new 30-second observation window immediately. When it is ready, the new comparison’s counters begin at zero. Earlier comparisons and their retained events remain available after Stop Monitor. Wait for the current baseline to finish before starting another; up to nine comparisons fit in one session. Stop and start another Monitor when that limit is reached. Spectra does not automatically judge that you moved or replace the baseline for you.

Finish and review coverage

Choose Stop Monitor for detailed results. The inventory distinguishes Since baseline, Starting baseline, and Earlier observations retained from before the current comparison. These sections describe observation time, not trusted or untrusted devices. Switching apps or locking the iPhone ends measurement. A late or recovering source gets its own 30-second starting window rather than producing a burst of apparent arrivals. Review Coverage for source loss and interruptions.

Each comparison keeps up to 40 recent raw change events, not unlimited lifetime history. Radio intensity is not a measure of risk, and a quiet dashboard is not an all-clear.
