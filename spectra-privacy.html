Privacy at a glance

This policy applies to the Spectra iPhone app and its pages on arcsignal.app, operated by Arc Signal LLC. It does not describe our other apps or the independent services you choose to use.

  • No account, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK in Spectra.
  • Signal analysis happens on your iPhone. We do not automatically receive your scans, device discoveries, notes, or magnetic readings.
  • Saved sessions are local. Spectra marks session storage for exclusion from device backups; iOS controls backup and restore behavior. You can turn saving off, delete sessions, or reset the app.
  • Exporting a report or contacting support is your choice. Files you export may be stored or backed up by your chosen destination. Review the recipient’s privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information processed on your iPhone

Depending on the measurements you select, Spectra processes Bluetooth advertisements and signal strength; supported Bonjour service advertisements on your joined local network; and motion and magnetic-field measurements. These observations help explain possible device categories, related signals, changes, and measurement limitations.

Retained evidence may include advertised names, service types, selected manufacturer or service payloads and product metadata, session-scoped identifiers, timestamps, confidence labels, coverage, and bounded measurement summaries or graphs. A saved session can also contain its name and notes you enter, plus the app build and catalog information used to interpret it. Advertised names and your notes can contain personal information about you or others.

Spectra does not capture camera images, microphone recordings, conversations, or geographic coordinates. It does not pair with discovered devices or open application connections to their endpoints. Local-network browsing can send discovery queries visible to other network participants; it is not invisible network activity.

The recognition catalog is bundled with the app and updated through app releases. Spectra does not send observations to an identification server or an AI service.

Only the access you choose

Bluetooth permission enables available advertisements. Local Network permission enables supported service discovery on a joined network. Motion access and available magnetic hardware support magnetic exploration during Area Sweep. You may skip setup or select fewer measurements; Spectra checks access again before scanning and explains unavailable sources.

The optional Show Wi-Fi name action requests Location permission, including Precise Location While Using the App where iOS requires it. Scanning works without Location access. Spectra does not request coordinates or save the Wi-Fi name in history; it clears the displayed name when the app backgrounds.

Review or revoke access in iOS Settings. Resetting Spectra does not reset Apple’s permission decisions. Measurement is foreground-only, not background surveillance.

Keep what matters. Erase what does not.

Completed sessions are saved by default in the app’s local storage. Turn off Save completed sessions in Guide to stop future history saving; existing sessions remain until deleted. History keeps up to 50 sessions within a 32 MiB budget, removing older reports when needed. Long-running measurement traces and event histories are also bounded.

Saved reports, notes, and AI analyses use iOS file protection. Spectra marks the session directory, including recovery copies, for exclusion from device backups. This is not a guarantee that sessions can never appear in a backup or on a restored device; iOS controls backup and restore behavior. Files you export may be stored or backed up by your chosen destination.

A privacy cover obscures sensitive app content when the app is no longer active. These protections do not make a report tamper-evident or guarantee protection on an unlocked or compromised device.

If a history file is damaged, Spectra pauses changes and may preserve a protected local recovery copy. Up to three recovery copies are retained. A temporary storage-access failure is retried and is not treated as permission to overwrite your history.

In Sessions, delete one report, select several, or use Erase All Sessions to remove saved reports and recovery copies. Reset Spectra in Guide also clears preferences and returns to Welcome. Both all-data actions remove app-owned temporary JSON handoff files; failures are reported rather than described as successful erasure. Individual deletion removes that report from primary history, not older recovery copies—use Erase All Sessions or Reset Spectra for those.

Erasure cannot recall copies saved outside Spectra, shared with another app or person, or retained by a service you selected. It also cannot remove mail you sent to support; contact us separately about that information.

Apple Intelligence and purchases

Analyze on Device is optional and user-initiated. Spectra passes the same evidence used in Analysis JSON to Apple’s on-device language model. This feature does not send scan data to Arc Signal, an external AI provider, or Private Cloud Compute. It saves the generated interpretation, supporting evidence references, analysis time, and version information with the scan. Choosing analysis also saves that scan when automatic history saving is off.

Model availability depends on Apple Intelligence settings, model readiness, language, and region. Saved analyses can be reviewed without generating them again and are removed with the corresponding scan, subject to the recovery-copy and external-copy details above.

Apple processes the one-time unlock and purchase restoration through the App Store. Spectra verifies the purchase on device to grant scan access. We do not receive your payment-card details or attach purchase information to exported scan evidence.

You decide when data leaves the app

Export JSON prepares a report locally. Analysis JSON contains meaningful retained observations, device claims, scan coverage, and user notes. The technical archive also includes retained raw data, graphs, and any saved AI analysis. Reduced identifying information omits names, notes, original identifiers, manufacturers, model labels, raw payloads, and absolute timestamps, but it is not anonymous: patterns and relative timing can still reveal context. Results filters do not restrict any export.

Save to Files and Share JSON use Apple’s system interfaces. A destination such as iCloud Drive, email, another app, or an external AI provider may transfer or retain your file under its own terms and privacy policy. Spectra does not send it to us automatically. Review the file, recipient, and destination before sharing; do not share information you are not authorized to disclose.

Support and diagnostics

If you email support@arcsignal.app, we receive your email address, message, and any details or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, and improve reliability. Start with a description of the problem, not private scan data.

Copy support information includes the app version and build, iOS version, hardware model, and catalog revision. It excludes scan history, network names, geographic location, and persistent personal identifiers. Copying is user-initiated, local to the device’s clipboard, and expires after ten minutes. Nothing is emailed automatically.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver this service. We do not use support messages for advertising. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

Apple independently operates the App Store and iOS diagnostic services. Depending on your Apple settings, Apple may collect or share crash and diagnostic information with developers. Any such information available to us is used for reliability and support. Spectra does not add its own automatic analytics or crash-upload SDK.

When you visit our website

The Arc Signal website uses no analytics scripts, ad trackers, contact forms, or embedded third-party content. Fonts and images are self-hosted. Visiting the privacy policy or Guide is a normal website request, not a scan-data upload.

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Its provider may process routine request information, such as IP address, requested page, browser details, and request time, to deliver and secure the site. Provider retention and processing follow the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Apple, your email provider, and export destinations have their own privacy practices. Provider processing may take place in countries other than yours.

Questions and privacy choices

You control app permissions, local saving, erasure, exports, and support sharing. We cannot remotely access, retrieve, or erase scan history that stays exclusively on your iPhone.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information you have provided to us, or to raise a concern with a privacy regulator. Contact support@arcsignal.app with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may ask for limited information to verify and handle your request; do not send scan history just to verify your identity.

Children

Spectra is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact support so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Spectra, our service providers, or requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current policy. Material changes will be explained here and, where required, through an appropriate notice or consent flow. The policy is available from Welcome and Guide → About in Spectra.
