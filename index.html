The Arc Signal approach

Clear evidence.
Thoughtful design.

Arc Signal builds Spectra to make security awareness more approachable. Useful observations, understandable results, and honest limits guide every decision.

01 / Focus

A clear purpose.

Start a check, review what was observed, and follow the evidence. Every step has a clear purpose.

02 / Privacy

Your data. Your control.

Spectra processes scans and Apple Intelligence analysis on your device. No account to create, no ads, and no analytics in the app.

03 / Trust

Evidence worth revisiting.

Recheck eligible saved sessions when app updates bring newer recognition. Keep the original and review a separate interpretation. No new signals are collected; a finding remains a clue, not proof of identity or safety.

Everyday awareness

New surroundings.
Thoughtful next steps.

Hotels, Airbnb stays, student housing, rideshares, gyms, changing rooms, and travel hubs can leave you wondering what is nearby. Spectra helps you review available signals—not certify a space or identify who is carrying a device.

Pair observation with practical habits: respect others’ privacy, protect sensitive conversations, and use trusted connections. A VPN can help with network traffic; it cannot stop a nearby camera from recording.
