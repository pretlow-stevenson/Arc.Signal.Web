Introducing Spectra

The signals are there.
Make sense of them.

An approachable measurement tool for the device you already carry.

Spectra brings together nearby Bluetooth broadcasts, supported services on your joined Wi-Fi network, and magnetic-field changes. Explore recognizable names, probable device types, and the evidence behind each result—without pairing with or connecting to discovered devices.

From smart-home products and speakers to camera, audio, and smart-glasses indicators, Spectra helps you ask better questions about what may be nearby.

An observation is a clue, not proof that a device is hidden, recording, or malicious. A quiet scan cannot guarantee that a space is clear.

Good questions

Know what a
finding means.

Can Spectra tell me a room is safe?

No. Spectra surfaces available electronic indicators, not a safety verdict. Silent, powered-off, wired, shielded, cellular-only, or locally recording devices may not appear. Review the measured coverage and use findings as a reason to look more closely.

Does a product name confirm the device?

No. Names and metadata can be changed or spoofed. Spectra uses its local catalog to present possible or probable identities when there is supporting evidence, and keeps the underlying observations available for review.

Do I need to connect to a device to inspect it?

No pairing or device session is needed. Local-network discovery does require joining that network and granting permission. Bluetooth observations and magnetic readings depend on the sources you select and the capabilities available on your device.
