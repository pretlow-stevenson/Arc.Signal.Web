Meet Spectra

The signals are there.
Make sense of them.

Three ways to explore the space around you.

See nearby Bluetooth broadcasts, discover supported services on your joined Wi-Fi network, and track magnetic-field changes. Spectra brings the results together, with the evidence behind each observation.

Explore possible device identities and camera, audio, smart-glasses, or smart-watch indicators without pairing with or connecting to discovered devices.

Signals are clues, not proof that a device is hidden, recording, or malicious. A quiet scan cannot guarantee that a space is clear.

A check for every setting

A quick look.
Or a closer one.

01 / Quick Check

Get your bearings.

Take a roughly 30-second look at nearby Bluetooth broadcasts, with optional local-network observations. No magnetic measurement or calibration is needed.

02 / Area Sweep

Explore at your pace.

Move through the area at your own pace, compare Bluetooth signals, and optionally follow the live magnetic meter. Finish when you’re ready, without a countdown.

03 / Monitor This Area

Notice what changes.

Keep the phone stationary. Each selected, available radio observes for 30 seconds to establish a baseline; you can extend this once to 60 seconds. Glance at change counts and the latest activity, then Finish for details. Foreground monitoring only—not an unattended alarm.

How it works

Look beyond a
single signal.

Quick Check listens to Bluetooth and optional local-network services. Choose Area Sweep for hands-on exploration with the live magnetic meter.

01

Bluetooth

Recognize available broadcasts. After a check, choose a found Bluetooth identity from grouped results and open Bluetooth Proximity for its live signal-strength graph. Less-negative dBm means stronger reception: −45 dBm is stronger than −75 dBm.

Signal strength—not an exact distance or direction.

02

Local network

Find advertised services on the Wi-Fi network you’ve joined, from speakers and streaming receivers to smart-home products.

Network presence—not a scan of every nearby Wi-Fi radio.

03

Magnetic field

Available in Area Sweep. See actual field readings, then compare changes from a stable reference. A higher actual µT value means a stronger field; a larger positive or negative delta means a bigger change from the reference, not a distance. Quality-gated spike detection uses its own calibrated baseline. Revisit the saved, downsampled graph after the check.

A field change—not proof of a particular device. Ordinary magnets and metal can cause changes too.

Names with evidence.

Explore possible device identities from the on-device catalog. After finishing, filter results by several device categories and measurement types and inspect the clues behind each match. Filters do not change saved evidence or JSON exports.

Start simple. Go deeper.

See related observations in Summary, then expand a result or use All Signals for individual identities and technical details. Groups are not confirmed device counts.

Focused glasses and watch checks.

Start a dedicated 30-second Bluetooth check for Smart Glasses or Smart Watches. No setup filters to manage—just supported matches and their evidence. Unclassified activity is clearly linked for further review. A match cannot establish listening or recording, and some devices may not broadcast recognizable signals.

Keep what matters.

Rename checks, add notes, replay saved magnetic graphs, and choose a full or reduced-information JSON export. Reduced does not mean anonymous. Learn the steps and limits in the Spectra Guide. Turn history off, delete selected sessions, erase all sessions, or reset the app.

The Arc Signal approach

Built for your privacy.
Respectful of others’.

On your device. Under your control.

Investigate electronic clues without handing your observations to a server. Spectra keeps its analysis local and explains the limits of what it finds.

  • On-device analysis. No account or ads. Analyze signals locally, with saved checks excluded from device backup.

Good questions

Know what a
finding means.

Can Spectra tell me a room is safe?

No. Spectra reveals available signals, not a safety verdict. Silent, powered-off, wired, shielded, cellular-only, or locally recording devices may not appear. Use findings as a reason to look more closely.

Does a product name confirm the device?

No. Names and metadata can be changed or spoofed. Matches are possible or probable identities, with the underlying observations available for review.

Do I need to connect to a device to inspect it?

No pairing or direct connection is needed. Local-network discovery requires joining the network and granting permission. Results depend on your chosen sources and device capabilities.
