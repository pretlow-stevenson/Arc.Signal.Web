Your observations stay on your devices unless you choose to share them. Broadcast names and payloads can contain identifying information. Review a report before sending it anywhere.

Save and organize sessions

Completed iPhone checks save locally by default. Change future saving in Settings → Save completed sessions. Watch sweeps import independently of this preference. An Area Sweep that reaches its time limit finishes through the normal workflow and respects this saving preference. Confirm important checks were saved before closing the app; an unfinished check is not a continuously autosaved draft.

Sessions groups captures by their original day. Tap a date to expand or collapse it. Use Session options → Rename for a useful name. Rechecks stay grouped with the original capture, rather than moving it to the recheck date.

Remember where a scan started

Enable Tag location before an individual iPhone scan if you want one location near collection start. It is off for every new scan, even when Location permission was granted earlier. A denied, unavailable, stale, or excessively imprecise fix never prevents scanning. Approximate tags show their estimated accuracy.

Scan start location describes the collecting iPhone, not a detected device or your route. Coordinates and estimated accuracy are available without loading a map or looking up an address. Watch captures remain untagged.

On your first tagged result, choose Enable map previews or Not now. Enabling allows compact Apple Maps previews to load automatically when you view tagged results. Declining is remembered without repeated prompts. Change Automatic map previews in Settings → Session maps at any time. Its marker and shaded area describe the collecting iPhone and its estimated accuracy—not detected devices or a verified room or venue. Spectra does not request your current position. Open in Maps is a separate choice for exploring the saved coordinates in Maps. No preview loads before enabling; opening Maps is always a separate action.

If the preview cannot load, your saved coordinates and findings remain available. Choose Retry map preview when offered. Regions unsupported by the preview, such as locations near the poles, do not offer retry; Open in Maps remains available. Spectra clears its temporary preview when you leave results or switch away from the app; returning can load it again while the preference remains enabled. Disabling automatic previews cancels pending work and clears the image. Reset Spectra clears the choice; deleting sessions does not. Preview images are not saved in sessions or exports. Apple handles map requests under its Maps privacy practices; clearing Spectra’s preview does not erase Apple’s service records or map caches.

Use Session options → Remove location to remove the tag from that saved copy. Independent rechecks and already shared copies are unchanged. If protected recovery copies exist, Erase All Sessions or Reset Spectra clears them.

Recheck with newer recognition

We regularly improve Spectra’s device catalog and detection engine. These improvements are included in App Store updates at no additional charge, with no separate catalog purchase or subscription. Install the latest app update before checking for eligible sessions.

A blue dot marks eligible evidence for newer bundled engine or catalog recognition. Recheck session creates a separate interpretation without changing the original, collecting fresh measurements, or uploading data. It retains the original capture context and any location tag still present on its source. Rechecking can work after you leave, but cannot recover evidence never retained or promise a better match.

Export a report

  1. Open Session options → Export JSON, or use Export results below completed findings.
  2. Choose one format: Analysis JSON, Technical archive, or Reduced identifying information.
  3. Location is excluded by default. If a tag exists, deliberately enable Include scan location only when you want to share its coordinates, accuracy, and fix timestamp. This also applies to the reduced format.
  4. Tap Export JSON. After preparation, choose Save to Files or another destination in Apple’s share sheet.

Analysis JSON includes retained evidence and notes. Technical archive also includes raw retained data and saved graphs. Reduced removes identifying text, raw payloads, and some collection context, but is not anonymous. Generated AI explanations are excluded from every format.

Exports follow the check type, not on-screen filters. General checks include all retained results for their scope. Specialist exports contain only qualifying findings; if none matched, findings are empty while coverage and check metadata remain.

Understand one finding

Analyze on Device explains a selected summary using Apple Intelligence when available. Explanations are temporary and excluded from exports. Copy for AI analysis prepares a concise prompt and one finding locally. The prompt asks what it could represent, what useful information the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, and which practical checks may help. Technical details are optional; review the exact preview before copying.

Location tags and your session notes are excluded from both AI evidence workflows. Names and payloads can still reveal sensitive information. Spectra does not upload to an AI service; pasting shares the copied material under the other app’s policies. AI conclusions are hypotheses, not verified detections or proof of recording, intent, distance, or safety.

Choose the right erase action

Swipe right on a session to reveal Rename, or swipe fully to open its name editor. Nothing changes until you save. Swipe left to reveal Delete, or swipe fully to delete that session.

Undo restores only the most recently deleted session, including its name, notes, location tag, and retained evidence. It is available for 10 seconds, or 30 seconds when VoiceOver is running at deletion. Another deletion replaces that opportunity. Leaving Sessions, switching away from Spectra, or confirming a bulk erase or reset ends Undo. Spectra removes the saved copy first and holds one temporary recovery copy in memory; there is no Recently Deleted folder.

  • Use Select to delete several sessions with confirmation.
  • Erase All Sessions also clears recovery copies and the received Watch capture inbox. It does not erase Watch copies.
  • Reset Spectra clears local sessions and preferences, returns to Welcome, and requests a reset of the known paired Watch app. Watch reset pending is not confirmed erasure; open Spectra on the paired Watch and check for confirmation.

Delete Selected, Erase All Sessions, and Reset Spectra clear pending Undo and cannot be undone.

System permissions and copies exported, emailed, pasted, or saved elsewhere remain. Deleting an original session does not delete its independent rechecks.

Storage is protected, but bounded

Local history uses file protection and is marked for exclusion from device backups; Apple controls backup and restore behavior. The primary history file retains up to 50 sessions within 256 MiB. Older sessions may be removed when a limit is reached. Export important records before filling history.
