# Magnetic measurements: website purpose and limits

The owner requested a clearer reason to care about magnetic measurements, not
another technical explanation. The website now leads with **Look for clues to
concealed electronics** and explains the conditional close-range mechanism:
some electronic components produce fields the iPhone may measure without a
recognizable wireless broadcast. Repeatable changes can guide a closer physical
look; they do not identify electronics, authenticate a camera or prove recording.

Ordinary magnets, magnetic metal, chargers and phone accessories can affect
readings. Some devices produce little measurable change; no change cannot clear
a space. Existing sensor-position, safe/non-invasive inspection, practice,
reference-versus-event-detector, saving and graph-retention guidance is retained.
No RF measurement, room-wide detection, powered-off-device finding, range,
security threshold or experimentally validated detection performance is claimed.

Purpose precedes technical details in the product's magnetic row, with the
limitations immediately adjacent. The real Area Sweep screenshot caption,
homepage card, support listing, magnetic article, getting started and travel
guidance reinforce the same value. Canonical support manuscripts and supplemental
mirrors are synchronized before public pages are generated. Existing article
identities, anchors and the promoted magnetic-help link are preserved.

This is a website-only copy update. CSS, scripts, wordmarks, screenshot pixels and
producer identities, native Guide export and App Store descriptions are unchanged.
The existing App Store description already explains the same bounded purpose;
the native app's functionality and installed build are not altered or rereleased.
Frozen Zendesk source is not maintained. Public help remains on Arc Signal.

## Evidence and review

- [NXP AN4247](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN4247.pdf) explains
  magnetic materials, permanent magnets, current-generated fields and distance
  dependence. It supports the physical mechanism, not Spectra detection efficacy.
- Apple's [raw](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmmagnetometerdata/magneticfield)
  and [calibrated](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmdevicemotion/magneticfield)
  field contracts describe local measurements, not device classification.
- [USENIX research](https://www.usenix.org/publications/loginonline/sneaky-spy-devices-and-defective-detectors)
  cautions against commercial detector claims. It is not a Spectra benchmark.
- [Apple Writing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing)
  and [NN/g plain-language guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/plain-language-experts/)
  support important information first, short paragraphs and descriptive links.
  The existing hierarchy and brand assets are preserved rather than redesigned.
